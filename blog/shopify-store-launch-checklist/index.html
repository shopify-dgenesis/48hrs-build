SHOPIFY GO-LIVE GUIDE

Shopify Store Launch Checklist Is Your Store Ready?

Verify the complete customer and order journey before removing your storefront password.

18 minute read Updated August 2026
IN THIS GUIDE Quick answer Six gates Launch checklist Launch decision FAQ

QUICK ANSWER

A store is ready when the complete buying journey works

A real customer must be able to find the right product, understand it, select the correct option, add it to cart, receive an appropriate shipping method, complete payment, and receive the expected confirmation without a launch-critical problem.

Optional animations, extra landing pages, additional apps, and advanced personalization can usually continue after launch.

READINESS FRAMEWORK

The six gates of Shopify launch readiness

Gate Core question Ready when
Catalog Are products accurate and purchasable? Data, prices, variants, media, inventory, and collections are correct
Customer experience Can customers find and understand products? Navigation, pages, product UX, cart, and mobile work
Commerce Can customers transact? Shipping, payments, discounts, taxes, and checkout work
Operations Can the business handle orders? Notifications, fulfillment, policies, inventory, and support are ready
Technical Is public traffic supported? Domain, analytics, performance, redirects, and search basics are addressed
Approval Has launch been authorized? One owner confirms blockers are resolved and scope is frozen

FINAL CHECKLIST

What to verify before go-live

Catalog and storefront

  • Titles, descriptions, prices, images, variants, and inventory are accurate
  • Collections, navigation, search, filters, and links work
  • Product pages answer key selection questions
  • Cart retains the correct variant, price, and quantity
  • Homepage, Contact, shipping, returns, and policies are complete
  • Mobile menus, galleries, forms, and controls are usable

Commerce and operations

  • Shipping regions, rates, profiles, and exceptions are tested
  • Payments, discounts, taxes, and checkout behave correctly
  • A complete test order has been placed
  • Order, shipping, and customer notifications are verified
  • Fulfillment, refund, cancellation, and support workflows are understood
  • Required apps work through the full purchase journey

Technical and search

  • Domain, SSL, DNS, and storefront access are ready
  • Analytics and important conversion events are verified
  • Titles, descriptions, alt text, and crawlable links are reviewed
  • Migration redirects are mapped and tested
  • Performance is checked after apps and media load
  • Placeholder content and test products are removed

Approval

  • Launch-critical scope is frozen
  • Known issues have owners and decisions
  • Critical and high risks are resolved
  • Post-launch enhancements are documented separately
  • One accountable person gives final approval
  • Launch-day monitoring responsibilities are assigned

GO / NO-GO

Launch-ready does not mean perfect

Fix before launch

Anything preventing correct product selection, cart behavior, shipping, payment, checkout, legal or operational requirements, or reliable order processing.

Move to phase two

Optional animations, additional promotional pages, advanced personalization, nonessential apps, and optimization experiments.

FAQ

Shopify launch readiness FAQ

How do I know my Shopify store is ready?

It is ready when representative customers can complete the intended journey and no critical catalog, UX, commerce, operational, technical, or approval blocker remains.

Should I wait until everything is perfect?

No. Resolve commerce and reliability risks; schedule optional enhancements after launch.

Do I need a test order?

Yes. A full test order validates checkout, inventory, shipping, payments, notifications, taxes, and order processing together.

What should happen immediately after launch?

Monitor orders, payments, shipping, analytics, support requests, errors, and real customer behavior closely.
