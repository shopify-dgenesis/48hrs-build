FAQ

Shopify Store Cost Questions

Quick answers to common questions.
Is Shopify free to use?

No. Shopify charges a monthly subscription, though trial offers may be available. Your final cost can also include a domain, theme, apps, and professional setup.

What is the cheapest way to start a Shopify store?

Start with the Basic plan, a free theme, a focused catalog, and only the essential apps.

How much does a custom Shopify store cost?

Custom builds commonly start around $3,000 and increase with specialized design, integrations, migrations, and functionality.

Are there any hidden fees with Shopify?

Plan for transaction fees where applicable, paid apps, domain renewal, premium themes, and ongoing marketing tools.

How much should I budget for apps monthly?

A focused new store may spend $20–$100 monthly; more advanced stores often invest $100–$500 or more.
