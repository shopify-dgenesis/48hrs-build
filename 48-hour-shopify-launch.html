Frequently asked questions

Shopify Launch Checklist FAQs

What should I do before launching my Shopify store?

Confirm your products, navigation, payments, shipping, policies, domain, notifications, analytics, and checkout through a test order.

Do I need SEO before launch?

Yes. Add clear titles, descriptions, image alt text, logical URLs, and analytics before launch so measurement and discovery start correctly.

How do I know my store is ready to go live?

Your store is ready when every critical workflow works on desktop and mobile and you can successfully place, fulfill, and refund a test order.

Can you help me launch faster?

Yes. Nehemiah offers focused Shopify builds for qualified, launch-ready projects with a prepared scope and content.

What are the most common Shopify launch mistakes?

Incomplete product information, untested checkout settings, missing policies, broken mobile layouts, and launching without analytics are common mistakes.

Is this checklist suitable for beginners?

Yes. The roadmap follows the launch process in order and uses practical tasks that first-time store owners can understand.
